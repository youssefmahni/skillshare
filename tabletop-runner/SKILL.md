# Tabletop exercises

Workspace: `/home/youssef/.openclaw/workspace`.
Full inject/AAR templates: `/home/youssef/.openclaw/workspace/skills/tabletop-runner/SKILL.md`.
Scenario library: `playbooks/tabletop-exercise.md` within the workspace.

Agree the scenario, participants, objectives and timebox. Mark every artifact as exercise material. Present initial conditions and reveal injects sequentially, asking who decides, who acts, by when and on what evidence.

Capture attributed decisions with exercise time and real timestamp; distinguish observed choices from facilitator assumptions. Probe communications, recovery, dependencies and applicable notification requirements without sending real incident notifications.

Finish with a hot wash and an AAR containing objectives, timeline, strengths, gaps, measured decision times, actions, owners and due dates. Record potential real-world follow-ups as recommendations, not completed control changes. Use the source's bilingual conventions when board-facing.
