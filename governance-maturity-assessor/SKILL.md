# Governance Quality & Maturity Assessment

**Operating Droplet:** `10.30.0.24` (`ciso-agent-governance-2`)  
**Lead Personas:** `GOV-QUAL-001` (Governance Quality & Maturity Assessment Specialist), `CISO-OFF-003` (Lead AppSec & Governance Officer), `AUDC-001` (Audit Committee Coordinator)

## Purpose & Scope
Measures institutional governance effectiveness, process repeatability, and oversight maturity across all 10 corporate governance domains. Benchmarks organizational performance against COBIT 2019 and CMMI frameworks, targeting an enterprise maturity score of $\ge 3.8/5.0$.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Governance Domain Scoring & Benchmark:**
   - Assess maturity levels across: (1) Leadership, (2) Strategy & Frameworks, (3) Policy Lifecycle, (4) Board & Committees, (5) IT Governance, (6) Cybersecurity Governance, (7) Data Governance, (8) AI Governance, (9) Assurance, and (10) Automation.
   - Aggregate scoring using standard CMMI levels: 1-Initial, 2-Managed, 3-Defined, 4-Quantitatively Managed, 5-Optimizing.

2. **KRI Computation & Trend Analysis:**
   - Compute governance key risk indicators: Policy Exception Rate, Delegation Bypass Frequency, Overdue Review Aging, and Regulatory Mapping Completeness.
   - Correlate maturity gaps with operational audit findings from Eramba.

3. **Bilingual Board Reporting:**
   - Synthesize governance health into executive-ready scorecards and visual radar charts.
   - Provide paired Arabic and English briefs highlighting strategic governance priorities, investment requirements, and compliance trends.
