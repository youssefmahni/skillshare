# Policy authoring

Workspace: `/home/youssef/.openclaw/workspace`.
Source structure and full template: `/home/youssef/.openclaw/workspace/skills/policy-author/SKILL.md`.

Read `playbooks/policy-author-template.md` and the specific framework sections required by the policy. Resolve organizational scope from current supplied facts and relevant workspace records.

Draft: purpose, scope, testable MUST/MUST NOT/SHOULD/MAY statements, roles, procedures, exceptions, enforcement, definitions, related documents, verified references and document control.

Include policy ID, version, owner, approver, classification, review date and evidence expectations. Use `compliance-mapper` for substantiated control mappings. Add Arabic sections for Saudi workforce/regulator-facing documents.

Save a new draft version under `compliance/policies/`. Distinguish draft, reviewed and approved status; do not invent approval signatures or effective dates. Preserve prior versions and describe the change set.
